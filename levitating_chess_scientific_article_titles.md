# Scientific article title list for the levitating-chess / planar-motor research

## Core articles we directly used or discussed

1. **Modeling and comparison of the Halbach array with different segments per pole**  
   Yunfei Zhou, Guangdou Liu, Rougang Zhou, Ligang Huo, Wuyi Ming.  
   *International Journal of Applied Electromagnetics and Mechanics*, 2015.

2. **Modeling of magnetically levitated planar motors with moving magnets**  
   J.W. Jansen, C.M.M. van Lierop, E.A. Lomonova, A.J.A. Vandenput.  
   *IEEE Transactions on Magnetics*, 2007.

3. **Magnetically levitated planar motor with moving magnets**  
   J.W. Jansen, C.M.M. van Lierop, E.A. Lomonova, A.J.A. Vandenput.  
   *IEEE Transactions on Industry Applications*, 2008.

4. **On Improved Commutation for Moving-Magnet Planar Actuators**  
   Yorick Broens, Hans Butler, Roland Tóth.  
   2023.

## Notes

- The most important articles for the actual chessboard actuator are probably titles 1–4.
- Titles 5–10 are useful for deeper Halbach/planar-motor modeling.
- Titles 11–16 are background/foundational references that may be useful when building the literature map.
- Separately from scientific articles, the important industrial systems to study are **Beckhoff XPlanar**, **B&R ACOPOS 6D**, and **Planar Motor / PlanarMotor** documentation.

# Next steps

Complete the virtual design before manufacturing or physical tests. Use the existing CAD, MuJoCo, magnetic, electrical and sensing implementations. The [simulator reference](docs/SIMULATOR.md) defines numerical verification; the [single-piece checklist](docs/SINGLE_PIECE_TESTS.md) defines flight-test coverage; [MODELING_APPROACH.md](docs/MODELING_APPROACH.md#physical-model-verification-gate) retains measured evidence and limitations.

1. **Integrate the numerically verified coil-field cache.** The bounded matched-drive comparison and nominal local movement pass; the [performance reference](docs/MODELING_APPROACH.md#next-performance-work) owns the evidence and scope. Integrate the cache into the existing magnetic backend with strict geometry/source/runtime identity and restart checks, removing superseded diagnostic overrides. Verify those integration changes without repeating the completed long comparison unless they change the numerical implementation. Preserve failed evidence and diagnostic-only snapshot restrictions. Disturbance recovery is separate controller qualification, not a prerequisite for accepting the cache comparison. Defer further performance changes and timing sweeps.
2. **Establish basic single-piece flight.** Follow SP01–SP05 in the checklist: settling, translation/braking, vertical movement, tilt and continuous rotation. Extend the shared reference generator without animation-specific controllers. Introduce the asymmetric knight alongside the king once basic references work. Keep existing noise enabled and add bounded disturbance/variation checks as each family matures. Perform an early static neighbour force/sensing screen before committing to the full campaign; it does not require a multi-piece dynamic controller.
3. **Complete ordinary chess movement.** Follow SP06–SP09: controlled coil/sensor handoffs, verified contact, takeoff, landing and continuous complete moves. Preserve failed contact evidence and unchanged limits until replacement verification passes. Resolve or bound inductance/PWM interference, current-readout timing and construction variation before adopting final hardware targets.
4. **Qualify combined movement and robustness.** Follow SP10–SP12 and the checklist's cross-cutting coverage: curved paths, simultaneous movement/rotation/tilt, oscillation and coordinated dance. Expand disturbance recovery, piece coverage, construction variation and defined fault responses. Revisit numerical verification for new mechanisms or unsupported domains, without repeating unrelated verification for every animation.
5. **Extend to the whole board.** Add neighbours, edges, capture buffers, shared-coil allocation and simultaneous sliding reset. Integrate power distribution, processing, communication, enclosure, cooling and the real-component BOM. Evaluate product workloads, manufacturing variation and faults, keeping single-piece levitation distinct from simultaneous sliding reset.
6. **Review the integrated virtual design.** Require reproducible sensor-based control, numerical verification for the intended use, credible uncertainty ranges and consistent mechanical, electrical and thermal evidence. Only then plan manufacturing and physical validation.

Develop thermal analysis alongside electronics and mechanics, recording currents, voltages and losses and feeding relevant temperature effects back into the model. Resolve winding fit and assembly tolerances before fixing hardware targets.

Before an expensive run, identify the unresolved property, acceptance rule and expected decision. Inspect existing processes/evidence, prefer recorded-data diagnosis, and preserve source/settings provenance, automatic stops and checkpoints. Wait for launched batches to finish rather than periodically inspecting unchanged progress. Never treat process completion as a passed simulation or relax limits merely to obtain a pass.

Keep only remaining work here. Quantitative settings belong in `Inputs`, `Fixed` and `Constants` in [model.py](../model.py), with descriptive comments. Follow the [organization rules](PROJECT_ORGANIZATION.md); reuse existing calculation owners and prune superseded guidance as work proceeds.

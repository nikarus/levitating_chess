# Next steps

Complete the virtual design before manufacturing or physical tests. Use the existing CAD, MuJoCo, magnetic, electrical and sensing implementations. The [simulator reference](docs/SIMULATOR.md) defines the architecture and finite verification checklist; [MODELING_APPROACH.md](docs/MODELING_APPROACH.md#physical-model-verification-gate) retains measured evidence and limitations.

1. **Discuss the first controlled-hover experiment before launching it.** The [frozen verification record](results/physics_verification/implementation_verification/summary.json) completes the implementation gate for bounded, direct-field, single-piece airborne investigation. Review that scope and choose a supported release/reference: nominal hover is currently at the lower verified gap boundary, so releasing there with zero current can immediately leave the range. Prefer an interior setup without widening limits. Keep direct magnetics initially; cached fields require matching refreshed evidence and an error allocation before use. Agree on the initial control experiment and its application-horizon numerical checks before starting it. This record is not a sustained-hover, hardware or all-motion accuracy certificate.
2. **Qualify sensor-based hover and disturbance recovery.** Use the verified physical model to establish sustained hover and recovery from bounded disturbances. Define realistic sensor interference, mechanical/magnetic disturbances and recovery requirements in the model; distinguish sensing interference from fields that also exert forces or induce currents. Include hardware uncertainty and noise realizations. Judge control against tracking, current, voltage and thermal margins. Retained sensor-count sensitivity tests support diagnosis but do not guarantee recovery from arbitrary interference.
3. **Qualify the required flight behavior.** Exercise translation, vertical motion, yaw, tilt and combinations, including acceleration, braking, difficult positions and coil/sensor handoffs. Test initialization, lost readings and recovery. Reuse the verified numerical method within its supported domain; revisit affected verification when introducing new mechanisms or exceeding that domain. Resolve or bound inductance/PWM interference, current-readout timing and construction variation before adopting final hardware targets.
4. **Add takeoff and landing.** Verify contact integration, then qualify transitions between surface support and airborne control, tilted approaches, settling and friction uncertainty. Preserve the existing failed contact evidence and acceptance limits until replacement results are verified.
5. **Extend to the whole board.** Add neighbours, edges, capture buffers, shared-coil allocation and simultaneous sliding reset. Integrate power distribution, processing, communication, enclosure, cooling and the real-component BOM. Evaluate product workloads, manufacturing variation and faults, keeping single-piece levitation distinct from simultaneous sliding reset.
6. **Review the integrated virtual design.** Require reproducible sensor-based control, numerical verification for the intended use, credible uncertainty ranges and consistent mechanical, electrical and thermal evidence. Only then plan manufacturing and physical validation.

Develop thermal analysis alongside electronics and mechanics, recording currents, voltages and losses and feeding relevant temperature effects back into the model. Resolve winding fit and assembly tolerances before fixing hardware targets.

Before an expensive run, identify the unresolved property, acceptance rule and expected decision. Inspect existing processes/evidence, prefer recorded-data diagnosis, and preserve source/settings provenance, automatic stops and checkpoints. Wait for launched batches to finish rather than periodically inspecting unchanged progress. Never treat process completion as a passed simulation or relax limits merely to obtain a pass.

Keep only remaining work here. Quantitative settings belong in `Inputs`, `Fixed` and `Constants` in [model.py](../model.py), with descriptive comments. Follow the [organization rules](README.md); reuse existing calculation owners and prune superseded guidance as work proceeds.

# Modeling tools and scope

Build123d and FreeCAD are integrated for the current assembly; see [cad/README.md](../cad/README.md). MuJoCo is integrated with the local magnetic, sensor and averaged-driver interfaces; sustained sensor-based flight remains to be qualified. Elmer and KiCad/ngspice remain planned tools. The [simulator reference](SIMULATOR.md) owns the architecture, general properties and verification checklist. This document retains tool scope, measured evidence and engineering limitations; [NEXT_STEPS.md](../NEXT_STEPS.md) owns the work sequence.

## Recommended approach

Keep Python as the coordinator of the existing calculations and MuJoCo as the motion/contact engine. Use build123d for geometry and Elmer FEM for spatial thermal analysis. Use detailed electromagnetic analysis for specific uncertainties, and KiCad/ngspice for the actual electronics. Complete the virtual design first, starting with a representative operating assembly and then integrating the whole board. Manufacturing, fixtures and physical measurements are deferred until that design review.

Build123d supplies geometry and CAD-derived mass properties. Reuse the existing magnetic, sensing, allocation, controller, electrical and heating calculations. MuJoCo replaces the hand-written rigid-body motion integration and adds contact handling after comparison checks pass; do not maintain competing production motion implementations. The current CAD magnetic screen follows prescribed motion and does not yet establish sensor-based closed-loop stability.

Prefer the least complex model that answers a design decision. Introduce a new tool or more detail when it resolves a specific uncertainty. Detailed simulation does not establish unknown material properties, supplier capability or actual cooling conditions; uncertain properties must remain bounded assumptions until later physical validation.

It transforms wire positions into the magnet assembly's coordinates, interpolates all three field components and rotates them back. The shared force, COM-torque and back-EMF calculations remain unchanged. The direct analytical evaluator is still the default and independent reference. The atlas benchmark keeps physical Hall sensing, coil-to-Hall maps and pose estimation direct, including a trial where only the controller uses atlas forces while the physical plant remains direct.

The implementation uses SciPy's [interpolating B-splines](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/generated/scipy.interpolate.make_interp_spline.html) along each grid axis and [tensor-product evaluation](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/generated/scipy.interpolate.NdBSpline.html). Samples remain below the canonical magnet assembly, avoiding internal field discontinuities; not-a-knot boundaries support interpolation near the top of this exterior domain. Queries outside the declared bounds, changed magnet dimensions/magnetization, nonfinite fields and excessive grid storage fail explicitly. There is no extrapolation or automatic substitution of the direct evaluator. The sampled domain covers this local flight screen, not all possible contacts, neighbours or future piece layouts.

The earlier partitioned-integrator [atlas benchmark](../results/magnetic_atlas/summary.json) selects a 0.2 mm quintic grid. Its 337 × 310 × 85 vector-field coefficients occupy 213.1 MB; this is coefficient storage, not total process memory. Direct grid construction takes 124.6 s and coefficient construction 8.7 s. Across 15 nominal, extreme and independent random combined poses, each with 137,025 wire points, plus 61,446 additional probes including domain faces, maximum sampled vector-field error is 7.61 nT against the unchanged 100 nT allowance. Force/COM-torque column and Hall-location field-gradient comparisons pass their separate limits. Field agreement is sampled evidence, not a uniform error guarantee throughout the volume.

Median force-matrix evaluation falls from 1.86 s to 0.361 s, about 5.15× faster. Three fresh adaptive 2 ms trials pass all existing operating and trajectory-comparison limits: direct evaluation takes 212.4 s; atlas controller with direct physical forces takes 201.5 s; atlas controller and physical forces take 75.4 s. The latter is about 2.82× faster before one-time atlas construction. Physical position/current differences in this short case are near floating-point roundoff; this does not imply exact interpolation or predict longer feedback behavior. Each trial has only one moving Hall delivery. Saved reports and checkpoints agree, comparisons reproduce, source fingerprints match, hardware current-sample times match, and sensor calibration/random states match.

The [coarser exterior-grid evidence](../results/magnetic_atlas_resolution/summary.json) retains the rejected 0.25 mm candidate, which narrowly exceeded the field limit. The [mirror-boundary screen](../results/magnetic_atlas_mirror/summary.json) and [setup failure evidence](../results/magnetic_atlas_failures.json) support boundary/domain regression checks. These are failed or rejected evidence, not additional qualified backends. No limits were relaxed to select the passing candidate.

## Error sources and qualification

Keep physical noise, fixed calibration errors, drift, timing errors, missing physics and numerical approximation separate. Numerical error is not a substitute for unmodeled physical interference: integration can add artificial damping as well as destabilizing error. Bound it through independent time-step and magnetic refinement while holding hardware rates and random sequences fixed. Do not double-count errors already represented in the sensor or plant. The accelerated adaptive comparison passes through 13 ms, including the previously sensitive interval. It is retained trajectory evidence; the general accumulated-error justification remains open under the simulator checklist.

| Source | Coupled model coverage | Remaining check |
|---|---|---|
| Hall noise and digitization | Correlated within-scan noise, native ADC rounding, sequential samples | Verify candidate component and ADC specifications; compare independent noise realizations and higher plausible noise levels |
| Calibration and coil subtraction | Fixed Hall/current offset and gain errors, coil-map mismatch, measured-current interpolation | Change calibration realizations independently; separate current sampling error from field-map error |
| Timing | Sensor startup/filter response, scan sequence, nominal packet delay and hardware update periods | ADC/multiplexer settling, jitter, asynchronous clocks, computation/transport delay and dropped packets under control |
| Power electronics | Sampled current regulator, averaged RL dynamics, command/current quantization, voltage limits | PWM ripple and aliasing, mutual inductance, ground/reference coupling, supply and return-path dynamics |
| Environment and construction | Nominal CAD king, coils and local sensor patch | Neighbour magnets, ambient/changing external fields, sensor alignment, magnet/mass variation, temperature drift and mechanical vibration |
| Numerical approximation | Explicit physics steps, magnetic quadrature, field/current interpolation and pose fitting | Independent refinement and repeatability; quantify impact on motion, estimated velocity, current and loss |

The Hall noise assumption follows the typical input-referred noise density and bandwidth in the [TI DRV5055 datasheet, electrical characteristics](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/drv5055.pdf). It is not a maximum-noise guarantee. Native ADC resolution and oversampling are separate: the oversampling target does not establish effective resolution when samples are correlated or rounding errors repeat. Current-sense noise/calibration and the ADC implementation remain provisional. Sensor nonlinearity, output loading and reference/supply rejection also need component/circuit evidence; arbitrary independent random noise would not represent these effects reliably.

After feedback stabilization and numerical verification, prioritize inductance and PWM interference: the existing [coil electrical screen](COILS.md) already exceeds the ripple allowance under its low-inductance assumption. The averaged flight model does not include that ripple. Resolve or bound its coupled force and Hall-readout effects with switching timing and sampling represented; do not replace it with unrelated white noise. Next bound current-readout timing, ADC/reference errors and calibration drift before broadening to neighbours and environmental disturbances.

First screen the estimator on recorded sensor data while preserving noise, calibration and delay. Compare candidates on several noise sequences and reject loss of tracking or excessive pose error. Then test fresh acquisition and the full feedback loop, numerical refinement, and joint physical uncertainty cases. Prioritize additional models using calculated interference and sensitivity bounds; do not invent amplitudes for unspecified hardware or claim coverage of unmodeled effects. All quantitative settings belong in the model parameter classes.

## Tool roles and automation

- **build123d and FreeCAD:** Python-defined geometry, assembly checks and manufacturing exports with visual inspection in FreeCAD. Generated STEP geometry does not replace the authoritative Python design history. [build123d](https://build123d.readthedocs.io/en/latest/), [exports](https://build123d.readthedocs.io/en/latest/import_export.html).
- **MuJoCo:** rigid-body motion, gravity, contact and interactive movement visualization. Supply CAD-derived mass/inertia and explicit contact/friction/damping properties; apply magnetic forces and torques through the external-force interface. Contact damping does not establish material deformation or internal structural damping. [Overview](https://mujoco.readthedocs.io/en/stable/overview.html), [Python](https://mujoco.readthedocs.io/en/stable/python.html), [simulation stepping](https://mujoco.readthedocs.io/en/stable/programming/simulation.html).
- **Existing Python/Magpylib model:** magnetic force/torque, sensor-field, allocation, electrical/control and workload calculations. Analytical magnetic solutions suit repeated evaluations, but their assumptions about current and magnetization distributions must be respected. AC current redistribution and material effects justify targeted additional analysis. [Magpylib physics and limitations](https://magpylib.readthedocs.io/en/stable/_pages/user_guide/guide_resources_01_physics.html).
- **Elmer FEM with Gmsh:** spatial heat transfer and selected electromagnetic or structural problems. Gmsh prepares the mesh and exposes a Python API; Elmer supplies the physics solvers. Scripts can generate solver inputs and collect results, but the project workflow needs a small integration check before expansion. Verify materials, boundary conditions and mesh convergence. [Elmer capabilities](https://github.com/ElmerCSC/elmerfem), [Gmsh](https://gmsh.info/).
- **KiCad with ngspice:** actual circuit design, circuit simulation, PCB layout and electronics BOM. Use available APIs, command-line operations and documented file formats; verify automation coverage for each task. Do not assume the entire schematic/layout workflow is supported by one API. [KiCad documentation](https://docs.kicad.org/9.0/en/eeschema/eeschema.html), [automation coverage](https://dev-docs.kicad.org/en/apis-and-binding/ipc-api/for-addon-developers/).

## Coupling and information boundaries

The [simulator reference](SIMULATOR.md#scope-and-information-flow) owns physical-state access, CAD mass-property ownership, event sequencing, magnetic/electrical coupling and visualization boundaries. Its [verification checklist](SIMULATOR.md#finite-verification-completion-checklist) governs evidence required before controller qualification. Do not duplicate those rules here.

Direct force evaluation caches fixed wire quadrature and evaluates fields in bounded worker batches without removing wire samples. Geometry or coil-membership changes invalidate that cache. The [performance evidence](../results/magnetic_performance.json) compares the implementation with the library and checks short coupled-trajectory equivalence; reproduce it with `python -m methodology.benchmark_magnetics`. This establishes an implementation optimization, not physical accuracy or sustained hover.

CAD and solver inputs consume the model parameter classes. External manufacturer simulation models remain identifiable source assets; adopted design assumptions must be traceable. Supporting work belongs under `methodology/`, with generated artifacts separate from source definitions.

## Thermal scope

The current thermal model uses simplified heat paths and thermal masses. It estimates temperatures and endurance but does not resolve the detailed spatial distribution through the coil assembly.

Begin with windings, insulation/potting, playing surface, PCB, thermal interface and baseplate. Include relevant magnet/piece contact or air-gap conditions for the chosen operating cases. Use a winding-pack approximation with effective material properties initially; resolve individual layers only when the question requires it. Bound uncertain contact resistance and cooling conditions instead of treating them as known facts. Check numerical convergence and sensitivity to uncertain material, contact and cooling properties. Physical comparison belongs to the later validation stage.

This work must explain or resolve the existing thermal failures. It must not replace them with a passing result through unsupported cooling assumptions, relaxed limits or omitted demanding workloads.

## Real components and BOM

Associate each selected electronic part with its manufacturer and exact part number, supplier reference, datasheet, footprint and, where available, a compatible simulation model. KiCad can export the selected component fields and quantities. Combine that electronics BOM with custom coils, magnets, mechanical parts, wiring, assembly and current supplier quotations. [KiCad BOM export](https://docs.kicad.org/9.0/en/eeschema/eeschema.html#generating-a-bill-of-materials).

A purchasable part, schematic symbol, footprint and validated simulation model are different assets. Check pin mapping, package dimensions, model compatibility and modeled operating conditions. Prices and stock require separate sourcing checks. Preserve the current model BOM as a labeled allowance until actual selections replace it; do not count the allowance and selected parts twice.
